Early access

ICGPT is in early access. Getting in takes two short steps.

1. Request access

Open the app and sign in with Internet Identity, then tell us how you'd like to use ICGPT — what you want to build or explore with on-chain LLMs. Your request is recorded on-chain against your principal.

2. Chat with us on OpenChat

Access is granted after a quick chat with the team. Join our OpenChat community and open a discussion — mention your principal id (shown on the access screen) so we can review your use case together and enable your access.

These docs are open to everyone. Early access only gates the studio itself — the Prompt Cost Lab and Chat, where you run prompts against the on-chain models.

Why early access?

The models run fully on-chain and serve one inference per canister at a time, so we're onboarding gradually to keep the experience fast while we learn how people use it. It also lets us talk directly with early users and shape the product around real needs.
